IoG RESEARCHERI'm researching how Appalachian food banks are moving into workforce development. What patterns do you see across these 12?
✦ How I reasoned
  1. Read the mission + program text of all 12 orgs (990 filings, FY2023–24)
  2. Classified each as food only or food + job training
  3. Compared revenue and program-spending ratios between the two groups
Job training shows up only once a food bank passes ~$250K in revenue.
3 of 12 pair food with training, and all 3 are about 3× larger than the food-only median ($95K):

IoG RESEARCHERIf a donor wants to fund $50K to expand job training, which is the strongest fit, and why?
✦ How I reasoned
  1. Weighed capacity: grant size as a share of each org's revenue
  2. Weighed efficiency: share of spending that goes to programs
  3. Checked for an existing training program to expand (all 3 have one)
Mountain Harvest is the strongest fit to consider.
$50K is 12% of its revenue, meaningful but absorbable, and it has the highest program ratio (96%)1. Hollow Creek would take a larger relative jump (18%), which adds dependence risk2.
