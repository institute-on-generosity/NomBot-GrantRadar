# NomBot

**Plain-language search over every US nonprofit, built on public IRS data.**
Institute on Generosity AI Fellowship · **Deadline: Dec 31, 2026** · Sister project: [GrantRadar](https://github.com/institute-on-generosity/NomBot-GrantRadar/tree/GrantRadar)

## Problem
IRS data on ~1.8M nonprofits is public but buried in raw files. Paid tools don't support plain-language search.

## Solution

| Phase | What | Planned | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Search** | Question → ranked nonprofits (EIN, location, financials, cause). Also a JSON API. | Nov 15 | ✅ **Built Oct 7** (local) |
| **2. Research Buddy (RAG)** | Chat with the results. The LLM reasons over the filings, shows its steps, cites every claim. | Nov 22 | ✅ **Built Oct 7** (local) |

**Search finds organizations. Research Buddy reasons about them.**

### Built beyond the plan
| Area | Feature |
|---|---|
| **Ranking** | **Relevance score** 0–100 per result, with Claude's one-line reason under **Why this result** (hover or focus) · strong matches first, weaker ones folded under "Show N weaker matches" |
| **Filters** | **Editable chips** read from the question: state, city, **Appalachia region**, budget (under $X, or **$1M / $10M or more**: established orgs for a small gift), cause, **graded cause label** (climate, women and girls: main focus by default, option to include partial focus; Oct 17), "must mention" · results update in ~0.4s |
| **Search** | **Suggestions** under the search box · **exact-mention** badges · **Google-style rows** (Oct 16): the name as the one strong element (link color, normal weight), one grey line of facts (place · cause · income with year · **finance status**: Growing/Shrinking, Surplus/Deficit as plain text after a small colored dot, amber not red, from the newest e-filed 990 when there is one; hover for the figures), a **two-line mission snippet**; Save, Compare, Why this result and IRS sources show on hover or focus · include tiny/inactive orgs · **CSV export** · **Print summary** |
| **Org detail** | **Cause labels** (climate, women and girls) with tier and a short why · **Website** (from the 990 header) · **Staff and volunteers** (990 Part I), All-volunteer badge · **years tax-exempt** (track record, from the IRS ruling date) · **where the money comes from** (Part VIII, government grants split out) · **this year vs last year** (Part I) · cash on hand, spending split (programs / admin / fundraising), liabilities · **leadership and governance**: paid leaders, board independence, audit and policy checklist (Parts IV, VI, VII) · IRS status · **financial health rating** (Strong / Medium / Weak) from fixed, visible rules, in plain words (Income, Bottom line, Debts, Donations), with **questions to ask** · **Grants received** (Oct 17): from foundations (990-PF), from public charities (Schedule I, split by DAF sponsor, community foundation, United Way, other) and **federal awards** (USAspending, by fiscal year, agency, program and place of performance), as three totals, one table by year, top funders and programs with Show more, each with a source note (Schedule I installments, federal fiscal year vs tax year, overlap between sources) · **Grants given**: Schedule I by tax year next to the return's own Part IX total, 990-PF, top recipients · **Print** as a 2-page memo |
| **Sources** | **IRS master file** and **SOI financial** viewers: every number links to its exact row · open beside the org in a popover · one side panel with a **tab per source** that pushes the page aside (doesn't cover it), closed in one step |
| **Analysis panel** | Results on the left (~60%); a sticky **knowledge panel** on the right (~40%): quiet text tabs with an underline, a **⋯** menu (Export slide), soft grey section cards (Oct 16). **Overview**, diagram first like Google's image strip: the headline with **one chart** picked for the result set (deficits, growing vs shrinking, or income sizes), then **Key facts** (label → value rows: related orgs, median income, share that spent more than they took in, foundation dollars + top funder), both computed from the data so they show before the AI text, then the **AI overview** card (2 cited bullets, the rest under Show more); **Track record** as one row (median years tax-exempt; open it for one bar per age bracket, click one to list its orgs with the year each became tax-exempt); **People also search for** (3 related searches, each runs on click); **Breakdown** (size, places, causes, team, **track record**: years tax-exempt; click a bar to list its organizations), **Map** (counties, Appalachia shaded; click a county to list its orgs; switch to **Gaps**: high-poverty counties (the poorest quarter of the map's counties, up to 20%; Census) with none of these orgs, listed beside the map; hovering a county on the map or in the list highlights it in both, hover its rate for a plain-language explanation), **Themes** (kinds of work, by Claude, each with its **foundation grants** in the newest three tax years: **median $ per org** (orgs with none count as $0), total, how many funded, the **least funded** flagged; on state searches, the share from that state's foundations), **Funders** (foundations that fund these orgs, from 990-PF grants; click one for the orgs here it paid and its profile; plus strong matches no foundation funds yet), **Finances** (growing, shrinking, deficit, low cash, mostly gifts; pick one to list the orgs) |
| **Research answers** | Aggregate questions ("how much government money goes to climate", "which states get the least per resident", "how many animal shelters in Ohio run deficits") get a page planned for the question: **research** questions open in the Research section (**`/research/ask?q=`**, sidebar Research highlighted, back link to the home page's Research prompts) as a **ChatGPT-style conversation**: one centered column, each question a right-aligned bubble, each answer plain content (bold answer sentence, key numbers inline, the chart, short bullets, a quiet icon row: copy, CSV, Export slide, How we got this), the composer pinned at the bottom with a one-line disclaimer, **New question** for an empty chat. Each **follow-up** ("and for women and girls?", "by state?", or one of up to 3 suggested chips under the latest answer: answerable next questions kept on the research question by rule, no cause nobody asked about, no wider place, nothing already asked, then ranked by a fast Claude call that can only pick from that list, streamed in after the answer) becomes a new answer, "why" questions stream a short AI interpretation from the numbers on the page (marked as such), a typing indicator shows while it works, and the whole thread lives in the URL (`&f=…`). Research Buddy is hidden there (the composer is the chat) and show only the **answer card** (headline, up to 3 tiles, one short chart with the comparison drawn in, up to 2 bullets that add something new), with no org search, landscape or AI overview run at all; **mixed** questions about a specific org set ("how many animal shelters in Ohio run deficits") show the card, then those organizations as the evidence; plain org searches and people keep their pages. The card also has what our data can't answer and where to look (e.g. USAspending), **How we got this** (cause set, method, specific caveats, coverage, every SQL query, sources), CSV and **Export slide**. 15 question types: total, share, X vs Y, trend, fastest-growing causes, by state (per resident), rural vs urban, top funders/recipients, out-of-state funders, grant size, general operating support, concentration, foundations funding both X and Y, org counts (deficits, under 3 months of cash, government-reliant). Sources: foundation grants, or government grants / contributions / revenue as reported on Form 990. Research Buddy uses the same engine as a tool for follow-ups ("and for women and girls?", "by state?"). Also answers **the largest 1% of nonprofits**: their share of all revenue (or contributions, government grants), and the share of foundation or DAF money they get. School districts and public colleges are kept apart from private colleges. Form 990 and federal totals for a graded cause count only orgs whose main purpose it is. **Never mislead** (Oct 17): any part of a question the engine can't apply (years outside the data, a place, a top-N set, a multi-year condition, per resident) becomes a visible "not included" note and the answer is marked partial; years outside the data are named in the headline ("Our data starts in 2022, so 2015–2021 isn't included"). Also **Appalachia** (ARC counties) as a place, **the N largest foundations' share**, conditions **N years in a row** ("deficits two years in a row"), and **household giving** (IRS SOI itemized charitable deductions by state, county, income and year, 2013–2023, after inflation) |
| **Research** | A feed of verified findings (`/research` and the sidebar; was "Insights". The home page **Research** tab is a prompt set like the others: example research questions that open their answer on `/research/ask`): funding gaps, rising and fading, money flows, track record, affiliations, financial stress. Each card: a short headline, **2–3 key points** (bold numbers, last one "Why it matters"; the one-line summary until a row has them), **Vega-Lite chart**, **Method** (method, caveats, the SQL), sources, date · one **Open research** button (or the headline) → the finding's page · **CSV**, **PNG/SVG** chart export, **Save** to a per-browser **Report** exported as one Markdown file with citations |
| **Research finding page** | `/research/<slug>`: **key numbers** (2–4 stat tiles), large chart (PNG/SVG), what it means (the key points), **data table** (every row, sortable, formatted, CSV), **organizations involved** (open in the org popover, **Search similar**), **Ask about this finding** (right-side panel like Research Buddy, opened from a floating button; answers from this finding's data only, general knowledge marked), method, caveats, SQL, sources, **Cite** (copies a citation), **related research**, Save |
| **Compare** | Pick 2–4 results (button in each row's right column) → side-by-side finances, staff, volunteers, mission |
| **Research Buddy** | Docked chat panel · **reasoning steps** shown · citations open the org · **chat history** per search |
| **Personal** | **History** and **starred (★)** organizations, sorted into **folders**, in a Claude-style sidebar |
| **Quality** | 👍/👎 votes (stored; hidden from rows for now) · **search eval** (50 questions, 91%) · **search benchmark** (`npm run bench`: keyword vs meaning vs hybrid vs OpenSearch BM25, before Claude scoring) · **Buddy eval** (30 questions, 1 unsupported claim of 401) |
| **Design** | Apple-style UI, **dark mode**, centered loading card with steps |
| **Partner API + MCP** | **API keys** (`npm run api-key`, shown once, sha256 stored, per-key rate limit) · `GET /api/v1/search` (`label=climate|women_girls`, `label_tier=primary|all`), `/org/<ein>` (with `labels`), `/research`, `/research/<slug>`, `/research/questions`, `/answer` · **remote MCP server** at `/mcp` (5 read-only tools, incl. `answer_research_question`) for Claude, ChatGPT and other AI tools · public docs at **`/api/docs`** + `/api/openapi.json` · no LLM calls, except `/api/v1/answer?q=` (one small routing call, ~$0.006 per new question; `?spec=` and MCP skip it) |

</picture>

[Interactive diagram](docs/architecture.html) · Self-hosted on the MacBook (Oct 16); Supabase + Vercel remain the cloud target.

- **Search:** question → Claude parser → filters + search text → one SQL + vector query → Claude scores the top 40, 0–100 with a reason → strong matches first, weaker ones folded. The query draws a few thousand candidates from indexes (2,000 nearest by meaning on a half-precision HNSW index, up to 1,000 keyword hits per source, up to 300 filings that state a required activity), then joins only those. One cached search (200 rows, 10 min) serves the results, "Show more" and the landscape. The embedding model loads at server start; `warm_search()` keeps the indexes in memory (every 10 min).
- **Research Buddy:** follow-up question → top 15 ranked results' 990 text → Claude reasons (steps shown) → answer citing [n] for every claim. No source, no claim.
- **Landscape:** the 200 closest orgs (same filters and ranking) → counts by size, city, cause, team, age (IRS ruling date); counties via `zip_regions`; funders via `grants`; themes by Claude. **Funding by theme:** one indexed query sums each org's linked grants in the newest three tax years (~10 ms for 200 orgs), then each theme adds up its orgs.
- **AI overview:** landscape (200 closest) → tiles + one chart, no Claude (`lib/overviewFacts`). The 20 closest matches in search order (not waiting for relevance scores; Claude is told some may not fit and cites only those that do) + computed figures (sizes, places, causes, team sizes, the chart's takeaway) → Claude → one-line headline, 2–3 bullets [n] (citations point into its own list), related searches. Runs alongside relevance scoring.
- **Export slide:** in the browser only: headline, key numbers, chart (Vega, light, off screen) and bullets → one 16:9 slide → PNG (canvas, 1920×1080) or PPTX (pptxgenjs, editable text). Nothing sent to the server.
- **Research answers (Oct 16):** question → free regex screen (plain org searches skip the rest) → **router** (Claude Sonnet 5.5, low effort, structured output, prompt-cached, answers cached 24 h) → a spec: metric, cause (graded label, else NTEE prefixes, else a keyword set marked approximate), money source, years, place, condition → **answer engine** (`lib/answer.ts`): fixed, parameterized SQL blocks on three slim views (generosity-data migration 033: `grant_clean` 4.93M grants, `grant_totals`, `flag_year` 777K returns), never model-written SQL → headline, tiles, chart and bullets from templates over the results. **Content plan** (`contentPlan` in `lib/answerSpec.ts`): research → redirect to `/research/ask`, card only, saved to `research_questions` (generosity-data migration 035: question, spec, headline, times asked; no personal data) for the **Asked questions** list (recent, most asked) above the findings on `/research` and the home Research tab (the question parse, org search, relevance scoring, landscape and AI overview never run; ~2 s new question, ~0.5 s cached); mixed (`count_orgs` with a cause) → card at once, then the org list streams in under it; anything the router isn't sure about → the normal search page. Research Buddy calls the same engine as the `research_answer` tool; the API and MCP return the same JSON.
- **Research layer (Oct 16–17, generosity-data migrations 038–046):** pre-aggregated facts the answer engine reads first, plus grant-size histograms for medians (046) and the company foundation flag (045). `grant_cube` sums every clean grant by tax year × recipient state × rural × NTEE code × recipient kind × recipient revenue band × foundation asset band × foundation type × cause labels (948,897 rows instead of 4.93M grants); `grant_recipient_year` (exact distinct recipients, concentration) and `grant_county_year` (county rankings). A **cause taxonomy** maps plain-English causes to all 681 NTEE codes (`cause_lookup`). New: a **breakdown** metric (by cause, NTEE code, county, recipient size, foundation size or type, recipient kind, year; dollars, grant counts or average grant) and **filters** on any foundation-grant question (recipient revenue, foundation assets, family-named / operating / trust foundations, school districts and colleges). Same numbers as the `grant_clean` queries (51 checks, `eval/answer-cube.mts`). Place answers say grants are **counted where the recipient is headquartered** and flag **DC** as an outlier. **Grant-size histograms** (migration 046: amount buckets per cube cell for foundation, Schedule I and federal grants) answer medians and percentiles. Schedule I lines that name no recipient stay out of the charity views and sit in `charity_not_itemized` (migration 050), which national `charity_grants` totals show beside the total. Refreshed concurrently by `refresh_research_layer()` in `db/refresh.sql`. [Data dictionary](https://github.com/institute-on-generosity/generosity-data/blob/main/docs/research-layer.md)
- **Inflation and benchmarks (Oct 17, generosity-data 052–053):** trend answers deflate by BLS CPI-U (`cpi_u_year`: calendar-year average for tax years, October-September average for federal fiscal years, base = newest annual average, now 2025) and show growth after inflation next to nominal, with a **Current / 2025 dollars** radio under the chart (a Vega-Lite param) and real-dollar CSV columns. National all-cause 990-PF answers read `benchmark_coverage` (IRS SOI grants paid, Giving USA giving by foundations) into Coverage and Method; national Form 990 totals show SOI's 501(c)(3) figure as context.

- **Household giving and never-mislead guard (Oct 17, generosity-data 054):** IRS SOI individual income tax files (county 2013–2023, ZIP 2013–2022, `etl/load_soi_individual.py`) → `soi_individual_county` / `soi_individual_zip` → answer source `household_giving` (totals, trend with CPI-U, by state, county, income class, per return, share of AGI, itemizer share). Router: regions ride on `area` ("appalachia" → `spec.region`, a separate nullable field made the structured-output grammar too large), plus `topN` and `streak`. Engine: `honorGuard` checks every spec part against what the SQL applied (`Place.used`, `ctx.honored`) and turns the rest into missing notes; sentence-style router notes are their own headline.

> Diagram out of date: it doesn't show the BLS CPI-U and IRS SOI / Giving USA benchmark loads (generosity-data 052–053) or the IRS SOI individual returns (household giving, 054) feeding the answer engine.
- **Regions:** ZIP → county lookup (`zip_regions`) powers the Appalachia filter (ARC's 420 counties).
- **Finances:** one rule set (`orgHealth`) for row tags, the Finances tab and the AI overview: the newest e-filed 990 (this year vs its prior-year column, cash on hand), else SOI + master file.
- **FLAG diligence (org page):** the newest 990's `filing_flag` row → fixed rules in `lib/flag.ts` (income, bottom line, cash, debts, donations, board, insiders) → red / amber / green signals, plus a question to ask for each concern. One deficit year is amber; red only when it repeats. Shared with GrantRadar. No AI verdict.
- **Research (Insights) feed:** a separate job writes findings to `insights` (chart spec, data rows, SQL, sources); the app shows only `verified` rows, best score first (`/api/insights`). Charts render in the browser with vega-embed, loaded only when a chart scrolls into view. **Finding page:** one cached row (`use cache`, hours) → stats, chart, table; orgs and related findings stream in. **Ask about this finding:** question → that finding's headline, summary, key points, method, caveats, key numbers, data rows (CSV, up to 60K characters) and orgs → Claude (Sonnet 5.5) → answer from the data, general knowledge marked.
- **Partner API + MCP (Oct 16):** partner or AI tool → `Authorization: Bearer nb_live_…` → route handler checks `api_keys` (sha256, 10 s cache), scope and per-key rate (in memory, per process) → the same data functions (`lib/api.ts`) serve REST (`/api/v1/*`) and MCP tools (`/mcp`, Streamable HTTP, stateless JSON) → daily counts in `api_usage`. Read-only, **no Claude calls** (search uses the local embedding model). No direct database access for partners.
- **Gaps (Map):** `county_need` (Census poverty, 3,144 counties) vs. where the 200 closest orgs are based → high-poverty counties (the poorest quarter of the map's counties, capped at 20%) with none, limited to loaded states.
- **Hosting (Oct 16):** runs on the MacBook for now, not the cloud (Supabase Large would be ≈$129/month). Production builds start at login as launchd services (`~/iog-host/run-app.sh`, NomBot :4000, localhost only), reached through **Tailscale Funnel** (a fixed public `*.ts.net` link to :4000). GrantRadar is merging into NomBot, so its :4001 service and the earlier Cloudflare quick tunnels are off. `web/proxy.ts` asks for `SITE_PASSWORD` (HTTP Basic) on every page and API when set, so a public link can't run Claude searches on our key. `/api/v1/*` and `/mcp` skip it (they need an API key), and `/api/docs` is public. Nightly `pg_dump` to `~/Backups/nombot` (newest 3). A fixed address and staff email login need a domain on Cloudflare.
