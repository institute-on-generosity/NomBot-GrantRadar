# NomBot

**Plain-language search over every US nonprofit, built on public IRS data.**
Institute on Generosity AI Fellowship · **Deadline: Dec 31, 2026** · Sister project: [GrantRadar](https://github.com/institute-on-generosity/NomBot-GrantRadar/tree/GrantRadar)

## Problem
IRS data on ~1.8M nonprofits is public but buried in raw files. Paid tools don't support plain-language search.

## Solution

| Phase | What | Planned | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Search** | Question → ranked nonprofits (EIN, location, financials, cause). Also a JSON API. | Nov 15 | ✅ **Built Oct 7** (local) |
| **2. Research Buddy (RAG)** | Chat with the results. The LLM reasons over the filings, shows its steps, cites every claim. | Nov 22 | ✅ **Built Oct 7** (local) |

**Search finds organizations. Research Buddy reasons about them.**

Research Buddy uses the same engine as a tool for follow-ups ("and for women and girls?", "by state?"). Also answers **the largest 1% of nonprofits**: their share of all revenue (or contributions, government grants), and the share of foundation or DAF money they get. > Diagram out of date: it doesn't show place tiers for city searches (`zip_places`, grant purpose word index, the In / Serving / Nearby / county groups), the city filter on research answers or the `/report` download (Oct 26), or graded labels used as search boosts (youth development, a girls preference) and as evidence for the relevance scorer (Oct 26), or the SDG cause taxonomy (`lib/sdg.ts`), SDG phrasing in the router (`withSdg`) and **Browse by cause** (`/browse`, figures cached on disk and warmed at server start) (Oct 26), or the **search summary** (Claude `search-summary` after relevance scoring, replacing the AI overview, plus the computed Did you know facts) (Oct 26).

- **Search:** question → Claude parser → filters + search text → one SQL + vector query → Claude scores the top 40, 0–100 with a reason → strong matches first, weaker ones folded. The query draws a few thousand candidates from indexes (2,000 nearest by meaning on a half-precision HNSW index, up to 1,000 keyword hits per source, up to 300 filings that state a required activity), then joins only those. One cached search (200 rows, 10 min) serves the results, "Show more" and the landscape. The embedding model loads at server start; `warm_search()` keeps the indexes in memory (every 10 min).
- **Place tiers (Oct 26, generosity-data 083–085):** a city question ("youth sports in Inglewood, CA") runs the hybrid search once per tier, each ranked and scored on its own: **In the city** (HQ mailing city or one of the city's ZIPs, plus orgs named for the city that mail from elsewhere, flagged), **Serving the city** (HQ elsewhere in the state, a Form 990 since 2021 or a grant purpose line since 2021 names the city, with the quote), **Nearby** (HQ ZIP within 3.5 miles of a city ZIP, Census ZCTA points), and **Elsewhere in the county** when the question names the county. A county question is one tier over the county's ZIPs. City ZIPs = the ZIPs where most orgs give that mailing city (`zip_places`). Tiers search by EIN list (every filing read by key, no HNSW walk), ~1 s uncached. In a small place, orgs with no 990 text join by name words or the topic's NTEE codes (`lib/topicHints`: youth sports N6/N4, youth development O, dental E3/E7), and recent $0 filers stay in. `org_service_places` is not used (its grade missed the bar).
- **Soft preferences (Oct 26):** "ideally", "especially", "preferably" make a preference, never a filter: a ranking boost (named in the text, or the preferred cause label) and a hint to the relevance scorer. A "must mention" needs a condition ("that do", "must", "only") and never restates the topic. Must-mention phrases match whole words ("van" ≠ "advance", "bus" ≠ "business"). Thin main-focus label results add the partial tier, with a note.
- **Causes by UN SDG and Browse by cause (Oct 26):** `lib/sdg.ts` maps the 17 UN Sustainable Development Goals → subcategories → what the engine counts (a graded label, IRS NTEE prefixes, or a keyword set), each marked label, NTEE, keywords or not covered, with UN targets; goals mostly run by governments and companies (6, 7, 9, 12, 14, 16, 17) say "limited nonprofit data". **Environment** is the umbrella over the Planet goals (climate a subcategory under Goal 13). Labels in progress (`environment`, `workforce`, `healthcare`) count by NTEE (C and D, J, E to H) until they load, then switch in one place. The router reads "SDG 4", "Goal 13", "quality education", "life below water" or "legal aid (SDG 16)" and the cause comes from the taxonomy, never the model's guess (`withSdg`, also on follow-ups). The home Research prompts sit in SDG blocks (number, short name, count → prompts by subcategory) plus four cross-cutting groups. `/browse` (sidebar, Research Report page): goal cards in the UN's five groups → goal page (the whole goal and each subcategory) → subcategory page: foundation $ 2022–24 by year, growth since 2020, orgs, top funders and states, concentration, money per org ranked against every cause (crowded or under-funded, with a link to the under-funded orgs answer), data years and ask links. Every figure is the research answer for the question its link asks (six answers per cause, `lib/browse.ts`), cached 24 h in memory and on disk, warmed 15 s after a server start.
- **Research Buddy:** follow-up question → top 15 ranked results' 990 text → Claude reasons (steps shown) → answer citing [n] for every claim. No source, no claim.
- **Landscape:** the 200 closest orgs (same filters and ranking) → counts by size, city, cause, team, age (IRS ruling date); counties via `zip_regions`; funders via `grants`; themes by Claude. **Funding by theme:** one indexed query sums each org's linked grants in the newest three tax years (~10 ms for 200 orgs), then each theme adds up its orgs.
- **Search summary (Oct 26, faster Oct 27):** right away, a computed line of what the search found (results, tier totals). In parallel with relevance scoring, the **20 closest unscored matches** (top of each place tier in turn) + facts computed from that same set → Claude → the description, worded as "the closest matches" and never stating the count (post-check `statesCount`). When scoring finishes: relevance-scored results → the strong matches exactly as the list counts them (place tiers: the rows shown; plain searches: every scored candidate) → computed facts (count by tier or by focus/cause, median income, income bands, foundation grants 2022–24 via `fundingOf`, name-only orgs, preference mentions, the 2 largest; `lib/summaryFacts`) → the scored count line (replaces the instant one), caveat and the panel's **Key facts**, no Claude. If under 60% of the orgs the description read are strong matches, it is rewritten from up to 30 strong matches in the background and swapped in. Claude Sonnet 5.5 (`search-summary`, low effort, system prompt cached) → 2–3 sentences + 3 related searches → **post-check**: any sentence with an org or place name or a number not in the inputs is dropped. Cached per question + matches (`search-summary:v3`). Same summary at the top of the CSV export and `/report`. **Did you know**: 1–3 facts with charts from the landscape (`didYouKnow` in `lib/overviewFacts`), no Claude. Replaces the AI overview (`lib/overview`, removed).
- **Export slide:** in the browser only: headline, key numbers, chart (Vega, light, off screen) and bullets → one 16:9 slide → PNG (canvas, 1920×1080) or PPTX (pptxgenjs, editable text). Nothing sent to the server.
- **Research answers (Oct 16):** question → free regex screen (plain org searches skip the rest) → **router** (Claude Sonnet 5.5, low effort, structured output, prompt-cached, answers cached 24 h) → a spec: metric, cause (graded label, else NTEE prefixes, else a keyword set marked approximate), money source, years, place, condition → **answer engine** (`lib/answer.ts`): fixed, parameterized SQL blocks on three slim views (generosity-data migration 033: `grant_clean` 4.93M grants, `grant_totals`, `flag_year` 777K returns), never model-written SQL → headline, tiles, chart and bullets from templates over the results. **Content plan** (`contentPlan` in `lib/answerSpec.ts`): research → redirect to `/research/ask`, card only, saved to `research_questions` (generosity-data migration 035: question, spec, headline, times asked; no personal data) for the **Asked questions** list (recent, most asked) above the findings on `/research` and the home Research tab (the question parse, org search, relevance scoring, landscape and AI overview never run; ~2 s new question, ~0.5 s cached); mixed (`count_orgs` with a cause) → card at once, then the org list streams in under it; anything the router isn't sure about → the normal search page. Research Buddy calls the same engine as the `research_answer` tool; the API and MCP return the same JSON.
- **City answers (Oct 26):** research questions naming a city ("how much foundation money went to youth sports in Inglewood, CA") get `spec.city`: grant rows by the recipient address ZIP (generosity-data index 085), org and filing rows by HQ. Answers that can't be split by city (state rankings, per resident) say they cover the county or state. No false "city-level data isn't in IRS filings" caveat.
- **Research layer (Oct 16–17, generosity-data migrations 038–046):** pre-aggregated facts the answer engine reads first, plus grant-size histograms for medians (046) and the company foundation flag (045). `grant_cube` sums every clean grant by tax year × recipient state × rural × NTEE code × recipient kind × recipient revenue band × foundation asset band × foundation type × cause labels (948,897 rows instead of 4.93M grants); `grant_recipient_year` (exact distinct recipients, concentration) and `grant_county_year` (county rankings). A **cause taxonomy** maps plain-English causes to all 681 NTEE codes (`cause_lookup`). New: a **breakdown** metric (by cause, NTEE code, county, recipient size, foundation size or type, recipient kind, year; dollars, grant counts or average grant) and **filters** on any foundation-grant question (recipient revenue, foundation assets, family-named / operating / trust foundations, school districts and colleges). Same numbers as the `grant_clean` queries (51 checks, `eval/answer-cube.mts`). Place answers say grants are **counted where the recipient is headquartered** and flag **DC** as an outlier. **Grant-size histograms** (migration 046: amount buckets per cube cell for foundation, Schedule I and federal grants) answer medians and percentiles. Schedule I lines that name no recipient stay out of the charity views and sit in `charity_not_itemized` (migration 050), which national `charity_grants` totals show beside the total. Non-cash transfers between related organizations (restructurings, mergers) stay out too and sit in `grant_transfers` (migration 067). Refreshed concurrently by `refresh_research_layer()` in `db/refresh.sql`. [Data dictionary](https://github.com/institute-on-generosity/generosity-data/blob/main/docs/research-layer.md)
- **Corrected NTEE categories (Oct 20, generosity-data 072):** `org_ntee_suggested` (4,049 orgs whose IRS code doesn't fit their own 990 text, a suggested major group and a quote, graded **94%** on 150; plus 42,973 orgs with **no IRS code**, method `uncoded_vote_llm`, a suggested major group from similar orgs and their own filings, graded **98%** on 150, and for 21,274 of them a **detailed code** `suggested_code`, graded **98.7%** on 150, generosity-data 96dcb5b). **Org pages**, `/api/v1/org/{ein}` (`ntee_suggested`) and MCP `get_organization` show it next to the IRS code. **Research answers** take `ntee: irs | corrected` (IRS default): with `corrected`, NTEE-coded causes, splits by NTEE group and fastest causes count listed orgs under the suggested code (`coalesce(suggested_code, suggested_major)`, so a detailed cause like K31 food banks picks up uncoded orgs with that code), with a footnote on the orgs and dollars that moved, and those **added** (no IRS code) counted apart. Joined at query time, no research-layer change: the cube reads a corrected union (cube plus each listed org's grants moved between groups), org-level tables test the EIN. Graded labels are unaffected.
- **Inflation and benchmarks (Oct 17, generosity-data 052–053):** trend answers deflate by BLS CPI-U (`cpi_u_year`: calendar-year average for tax years, October-September average for federal fiscal years, base = newest annual average, now 2025) and show growth after inflation next to nominal, with a **Current / 2025 dollars** radio under the chart (a Vega-Lite param) and real-dollar CSV columns.
