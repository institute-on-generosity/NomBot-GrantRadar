# NomBot

**Plain-language search over every US nonprofit, built on public IRS data.**
Institute on Generosity AI Fellowship · **Deadline: Dec 31, 2026** · Sister project: [GrantRadar](https://github.com/institute-on-generosity/NomBot-GrantRadar/tree/GrantRadar)

## Problem
IRS data on ~1.8M nonprofits is public but buried in raw files. Paid tools don't support plain-language search.

## Solution

| Phase | What | Planned | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Search** | Question → ranked nonprofits (EIN, location, financials, cause). Also a JSON API. | Nov 15 | ✅ **Built Oct 7** (local) |
| **2. Research Buddy (RAG)** | Chat with the results. The LLM reasons over the filings, shows its steps, cites every claim. | Nov 22 | ✅ **Built Oct 7** (local) |

**Search finds organizations. Research Buddy reasons about them.**

| **Research answers** | Aggregate questions ("how much government money goes to climate", "which states get the least per resident", "how many animal shelters in Ohio run deficits") get a page planned for the question: **research** questions open in the Research section (**`/research/ask?q=`**, sidebar Research highlighted, back link to the home page's Research prompts) as a **ChatGPT-style conversation**: one centered column, each question a right-aligned bubble, each answer plain content (bold answer sentence, key numbers inline, the chart, short bullets, a quiet icon row: copy, CSV, Export slide, How we got this), the composer pinned at the bottom with a one-line disclaimer, **New question** for an empty chat. Each **follow-up** ("and for women and girls?", "by state?", or one of up to 3 suggested chips under the latest answer: answerable next questions kept on the research question by rule, no cause nobody asked about, no wider place, nothing already asked, then ranked by a fast Claude call that can only pick from that list, streamed in after the answer) becomes a new answer, "why" questions stream a short AI interpretation from the numbers on the page (marked as such), a typing indicator shows while it works, and the whole thread lives in the URL (`&f=…`). Research Buddy is hidden there (the composer is the chat) and show only the **answer card** (headline, up to 3 tiles, one short chart with the comparison drawn in, up to 2 bullets that add something new), with no org search, landscape or AI overview run at all; **mixed** questions about a specific org set ("how many animal shelters in Ohio run deficits") show the card, then those organizations as the evidence; plain org searches and people keep their pages. The card also has what our data can't answer and where to look (e.g. USAspending), **How we got this** (cause set, method, specific caveats, coverage, every SQL query, sources), CSV and **Export slide**. 15 question types: total, share, X vs Y, trend, fastest-growing causes, by state (per resident), rural vs urban, top funders/recipients, out-of-state funders, grant size, general operating support, concentration, foundations funding both X and Y, org counts (deficits, under 3 months of cash, government-reliant). Sources: foundation grants, or government grants / contributions / revenue as reported on Form 990. Research Buddy uses the same engine as a tool for follow-ups ("and for women and girls?", "by state?"). Also answers **the largest 1% of nonprofits**: their share of all revenue (or contributions, government grants), and the share of foundation or DAF money they get. School districts and public colleges are kept apart from private colleges. Form 990 and federal totals for a graded cause count only orgs whose main purpose it is. **Never mislead** (Oct 17): any part of a question the engine can't apply (years outside the data, a place, a top-N set, a multi-year condition, per resident) becomes a visible "not included" note and the answer is marked partial; years outside the data are named in the headline ("Our data starts in 2022, so 2015–2021 isn't included"). Also **Appalachia** (ARC counties) as a place, **the N largest foundations' share**, conditions **N years in a row** ("deficits two years in a row"), and **household giving** (IRS SOI itemized charitable deductions by state, county, income and year, 2013–2023, after inflation). **Foundation payout** (Oct 17): median payout rate against the 5% rule, share paying 5% or more, by asset size, foundation type, state and year, with assets. **Nonprofit finances over time**: share running deficits (or thin cash, government reliance) by year, with the same orgs every year alongside, by revenue size, deficit streaks, median months of cash. **Two causes at once** (Oct 19): "which foundations fund climate, and which fund women and girls" ranks both (one chart with both lists, a tile and a bullet per cause) and adds the overlap ("7 of the top 50 climate funders are also top 50 for women and girls, led by Hewlett"); top recipients the same. A cause the answer's SQL didn't cover is always a "not included" note. Foundation names read in full ("Fdn" → Foundation, "Chrtbl Tr" → Charitable Trust). **Show the steps** (Oct 20): a numbered, collapsed evidence chain under every answer (question read, cause set, data, per-year totals with the biggest recipients and funders linked, the arithmetic, the baseline, checks, SQL), built from the same query results with no extra Claude call, also in the API and MCP. A trend whose start year spiked (over 30% above the year before) says so in a bullet and offers **Since 2021?** |

[Interactive diagram](docs/architecture.html) · Self-hosted on the MacBook (Oct 16); Supabase + Vercel remain the cloud target.

- **Search:** question → Claude parser → filters + search text → one SQL + vector query → Claude scores the top 40, 0–100 with a reason → strong matches first, weaker ones folded. The query draws a few thousand candidates from indexes (2,000 nearest by meaning on a half-precision HNSW index, up to 1,000 keyword hits per source, up to 300 filings that state a required activity), then joins only those. One cached search (200 rows, 10 min) serves the results, "Show more" and the landscape. The embedding model loads at server start; `warm_search()` keeps the indexes in memory (every 10 min).
- **Research Buddy:** follow-up question → top 15 ranked results' 990 text → Claude reasons (steps shown) → answer citing [n] for every claim. No source, no claim.
- **Landscape:** the 200 closest orgs (same filters and ranking) → counts by size, city, cause, team, age (IRS ruling date); counties via `zip_regions`; funders via `grants`; themes by Claude. **Funding by theme:** one indexed query sums each org's linked grants in the newest three tax years (~10 ms for 200 orgs), then each theme adds up its orgs.
- **AI overview:** landscape (200 closest) → tiles + one chart, no Claude (`lib/overviewFacts`). The 20 closest matches in search order (not waiting for relevance scores; Claude is told some may not fit and cites only those that do) + computed figures (sizes, places, causes, team sizes, the chart's takeaway) → Claude → one-line headline, 2–3 bullets [n] (citations point into its own list), related searches. Runs alongside relevance scoring.
- **Export slide:** in the browser only: headline, key numbers, chart (Vega, light, off screen) and bullets → one 16:9 slide → PNG (canvas, 1920×1080) or PPTX (pptxgenjs, editable text). Nothing sent to the server.
- **Research answers (Oct 16):** question → free regex screen (plain org searches skip the rest) → **router** (Claude Sonnet 5.5, low effort, structured output, prompt-cached, answers cached 24 h) → a spec: metric, cause (graded label, else NTEE prefixes, else a keyword set marked approximate), money source, years, place, condition → **answer engine** (`lib/answer.ts`): fixed, parameterized SQL blocks on three slim views (generosity-data migration 033: `grant_clean` 4.93M grants, `grant_totals`, `flag_year` 777K returns), never model-written SQL → headline, tiles, chart and bullets from templates over the results. **Content plan** (`contentPlan` in `lib/answerSpec.ts`): research → redirect to `/research/ask`, card only, saved to `research_questions` (generosity-data migration 035: question, spec, headline, times asked; no personal data) for the **Asked questions** list (recent, most asked) above the findings on `/research` and the home Research tab (the question parse, org search, relevance scoring, landscape and AI overview never run; ~2 s new question, ~0.5 s cached); mixed (`count_orgs` with a cause) → card at once, then the org list streams in under it; anything the router isn't sure about → the normal search page. Research Buddy calls the same engine as the `research_answer` tool; the API and MCP return the same JSON.
- **Research layer (Oct 16–17, generosity-data migrations 038–046):** pre-aggregated facts the answer engine reads first, plus grant-size histograms for medians (046) and the company foundation flag (045). `grant_cube` sums every clean grant by tax year × recipient state × rural × NTEE code × recipient kind × recipient revenue band × foundation asset band × foundation type × cause labels (948,897 rows instead of 4.93M grants); `grant_recipient_year` (exact distinct recipients, concentration) and `grant_county_year` (county rankings). A **cause taxonomy** maps plain-English causes to all 681 NTEE codes (`cause_lookup`). New: a **breakdown** metric (by cause, NTEE code, county, recipient size, foundation size or type, recipient kind, year; dollars, grant counts or average grant) and **filters** on any foundation-grant question (recipient revenue, foundation assets, family-named / operating / trust foundations, school districts and colleges). Same numbers as the `grant_clean` queries (51 checks, `eval/answer-cube.mts`). Place answers say grants are **counted where the recipient is headquartered** and flag **DC** as an outlier. **Grant-size histograms** (migration 046: amount buckets per cube cell for foundation, Schedule I and federal grants) answer medians and percentiles. Schedule I lines that name no recipient stay out of the charity views and sit in `charity_not_itemized` (migration 050), which national `charity_grants` totals show beside the total. Non-cash transfers between related organizations (restructurings, mergers) stay out too and sit in `grant_transfers` (migration 067). Refreshed concurrently by `refresh_research_layer()` in `db/refresh.sql`. [Data dictionary](https://github.com/institute-on-generosity/generosity-data/blob/main/docs/research-layer.md)
- **Corrected NTEE categories (Oct 20, generosity-data 072):** `org_ntee_suggested` (4,049 orgs whose IRS code doesn't fit their own 990 text, a suggested major group and a quote, graded **94%** on 150; plus 42,973 orgs with **no IRS code**, method `uncoded_vote_llm`, a suggested major group from similar orgs and their own filings, graded **98%** on 150). **Org pages**, `/api/v1/org/{ein}` (`ntee_suggested`) and MCP `get_organization` show it next to the IRS code. **Research answers** take `ntee: irs | corrected` (IRS default): with `corrected`, NTEE-coded causes, splits by NTEE group and fastest causes count listed orgs under the suggested group, with a footnote on the orgs and dollars that moved, and those **added** (no IRS code) counted apart. Joined at query time, no research-layer change: the cube reads a corrected union (cube plus each listed org's grants moved between groups), org-level tables test the EIN. Graded labels are unaffected.
- **Inflation and benchmarks (Oct 17, generosity-data 052–053):** trend answers deflate by BLS CPI-U (`cpi_u_year`: calendar-year average for tax years, October-September average for federal fiscal years, base = newest annual average, now 2025) and show growth after inflation next to nominal, with a **Current / 2025 dollars** radio under the chart (a Vega-Lite param) and real-dollar CSV columns. National all-cause 990-PF answers read `benchmark_coverage` (IRS SOI grants paid, Giving USA giving by foundations) into Coverage and Method; national Form 990 totals show SOI's 501(c)(3) figure as context.

- **Household giving and never-mislead guard (Oct 17, generosity-data 054):** IRS SOI individual income tax files (county 2013–2023, ZIP 2013–2022, `etl/load_soi_individual.py`) → `soi_individual_county` / `soi_individual_zip` → answer source `household_giving` (totals, trend with CPI-U, by state, county, income class, per return, share of AGI, itemizer share). Router: regions ride on `area` ("appalachia" → `spec.region`, a separate nullable field made the structured-output grammar too large), plus `topN` and `streak`. Engine: `honorGuard` checks every spec part against what the SQL applied (`Place.used`, `ctx.honored`) and turns the rest into missing notes; sentence-style router notes are their own headline.

- **Finance panels (Oct 17, generosity-data 055–056):** 990-PF XML → `etl/load_foundation_finances.py` → `foundation_finances` (one row per foundation per year, newest return wins) → answer source `foundation_finances` (payout one year, by size / type / state, trend with assets). `filing_flag` → `org_finance_year` (own 990 or the next year's prior-year column) → `count_orgs` by year or revenue size, `trend` with a condition. Router: one new source value, no new spec fields (grammar limit).
- **Under-funded orgs (Oct 21):** metric `underfunded` → `org_finance_year` newest full Form 990 + `filing_flag` (program expenses, prior-year revenue, salaries) for the cause → peers = same cause and revenue band nationwide → 990-PF grants a year per revenue dollar (`grant_clean`) against the peer median, Schedule I grants (`charity_grant_clean`) as a screen → the top 40 shortfalls checked for unlinked grants under a similar name in their state (`grants`, pg_trgm) → up to 10 orgs in `Answer.orgs` (API `underfunded_orgs`), shown as a table linked to org pages.

- **Regions:** ZIP → county lookup (`zip_regions`) powers the Appalachia filter (ARC's 420 counties).
- **Finances:** one rule set (`orgHealth`) for row tags, the Finances tab and the AI overview: the newest e-filed 990 (this year vs its prior-year column, cash on hand), else SOI + master file.
- **FLAG diligence (org page):** the newest 990's `filing_flag` row → fixed rules in `lib/flag.ts` (income, bottom line, cash, debts, donations, board, insiders) → red / amber / green signals, plus a question to ask for each concern. One deficit year is amber; red only when it repeats. Shared with GrantRadar. No AI verdict.
- **Research (Insights) feed:** a separate job writes findings to `insights` (chart spec, data rows, SQL, sources); the app shows only `verified` rows, best score first (`/api/insights`). Charts render in the browser with vega-embed, loaded only when a chart scrolls into view. **Finding page:** one cached row (`use cache`, hours) → stats, chart, table; orgs and related findings stream in. **Ask about this finding:** question → that finding's headline, summary, key points, method, caveats, key numbers, data rows (CSV, up to 60K characters) and orgs → Claude (Sonnet 5.5) → answer from the data, general knowledge marked.
- **Partner API + MCP (Oct 16):** partner or AI tool → `Authorization: Bearer nb_live_…` → route handler checks `api_keys` (sha256, 10 s cache), scope and per-key rate (in memory, per process) → the same data functions (`lib/api.ts`, row lists in `lib/apiData.ts`) serve REST (`/api/v1/*`) and MCP tools (`/mcp`, Streamable HTTP, stateless JSON) → daily counts in `api_usage`. Row lists page newest first with an opaque keyset cursor (no OFFSET) on generosity-data 058's `(tax_year, id)` and `(state, tax_year, id)` indexes; grants to individuals withhold the name and ZIP. Read-only, **no Claude calls** (search uses the local embedding model). No direct database access for partners.
